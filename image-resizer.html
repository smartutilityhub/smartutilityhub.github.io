Free Image Resizer Online

Resize your JPG, JPEG or PNG image by changing width and height. Fast, simple and completely free.

How to Resize an Image?

  1. Click Upload Your Image.
  2. Select a JPG, JPEG or PNG image.
  3. Enter your required width or height in pixels.
  4. Click Resize Image.
  5. Click Download Image to save it.
